### IMG133

$$
\iint_R = \int_{-1}^1 (\sqrt{1+y^2} - |y|) \, dy = 2 \int_0^1 (\sqrt{1+y^2} - y) \, dy.
$$

由 $\int_0^1 \sqrt{1+y^2} \, dy = \frac{1}{2} \left[ y\sqrt{1+y^2} + \ln(y + \sqrt{1+y^2}) \right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{2} (\sqrt{2} + \ln(1 + \sqrt{2})), \int_0^1 y \, dy = \frac{1}{2}$

$$
\iint_R = 2 \left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{\ln(1 + \sqrt{2})}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \right) = \sqrt{2} + \ln(1 + \sqrt{2}) - 1.
$$
