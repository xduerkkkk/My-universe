
 
 *   `vividly illustrated in this chart is...` $\rightarrow$ **漂亮！** 图表题把母版的 `depicted in the cartoon` 换成 `illustrated in the bar chart`，非常地道！
*   `enhance the skill in current works?` $\rightarrow$ **注意坑！** “工作”作“职业/岗位”讲时，`work` 不可数；加 `s` 的 `works` 意思是“工厂”或“文学作品”。地道表达是 **`improve skills for their current jobs`**（或 `positions`）。
*   `Fewer aim to merely get the certificate.` $\rightarrow$ 意思全对！稍微润色成复数/更正式：`far fewer people aim to merely obtain diplomas or professional certificates.`
*   `real progress for themselves` $\rightarrow$ 想要表达“真正的自我提升”，考研高分替换词叫 **`substantive self-improvement`** 或 **`practical competence`**（实在太难背，就用你母版 1 里背过的 **`personal fulfillment`** 或 **`accumulate knowledge`**！）。
