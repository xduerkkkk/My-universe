
| 归并类别          | 涵盖的具体题型              | 底层逻辑                                                  |
| :------------ | :------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. 诉求与解决类** | **建议信、投诉信、咨询信**      | 说明问题/现状 $\to$ 提出 2~3 点具体建议或要求 $\to$ 期待改进或答复           |
| **2. 人际与情谊类** | **感谢信、道歉信、邀请信、祝贺信**  | 表达情绪（谢/歉/喜/邀） $\to$ 阐述细节/原因 $\to$ 再次表达心意与美好祝愿         |
| **3. 事务与申请类** | **申请/求职信、辞职信、推荐信**   | 说明申请/辞职/推荐意图 $\to$ 列举能力、资质或客观理由 $\to$ 期待面试/批准         |
| **4. 公开告示类**  | **Notice（通知）、招募、倡议** | 格式是通知排版，内容为：活动背景/目的 $\to$ 时间/地点/要求/流程 $\to$ 呼吁参与/联系方式 |

# 第一类诉求建议 

**第一段：精准破题（区分对公/对私）**

- **对公（机构/校长/部门）**：I am writing this letter to bring an issue to your attention and respectfully put forward two suggestions regarding [主题名词短语/动名词]. _(注：既然第二段固定写 2 点，建议把对公的 several 也统一改为 two，与对私完全同步)_
    
- **对私（朋友/同学/外教）**：I am delighted to receive your email inquiring about [主题]. I am writing to share two practical suggestions for your reference.
    

**第二段：主体展开（合为一个自然段！只写 2 点，主攻好处）** First and foremost, **[对公：it would be highly beneficial if you could + 动词原形 / 对私：it is advisable for you to + 动词原形]** [建议一的做法]. This approach will effectively [建议一的好处 - 动词原形]. Moreover, **[对公：steps should also be taken to + 动词原形 / 对私：you may also consider + 动名词 doing]** [建议二的做法]. Specifically, beyond [好处 A - 动名词], this measure will also significantly [好处 B - 动词原形], which is conducive to [长远效果 - 名词短语/动名词].

**第三段：礼貌收尾（区分对公/对私）**

- **对公**：I sincerely hope that you will take these suggestions into consideration. Thank you for your time and attention.
- **对私**：I sincerely hope that you will find these suggestions helpful. Please feel free to reach out if you need further help.



#### 1. 24 年真题（对私版：给朋友 Paul 建议古代科学家口头报告）

Dear Paul, I am delighted to receive your email inquiring about the oral report on ancient Chinese scientists.
I am writing to share two practical suggestions for your reference. First and foremost, **it is advisable for you to** focus on the story of Zu Chongzhi, a highly representative mathematician in ancient China. This approach will effectively make your report both informative and culturally profound. Moreover, **you may also consider** enriching the content via the medium of short videos and practicing several times beforehand. Specifically, beyond **making your presentation more engaging to the audience**, this measure will also significantly **help you manage your speech time**, which is conducive to **building up your confidence and composure on stage**. I sincerely hope that you will find these suggestions helpful. Please feel free to reach out if you need further help. Yours sincerely, Li Ming


#### 2. 14 年真题（对公版：给校长建议提高学生身体素质）

Dear President,
I am writing this letter to bring an issue to your attention and respectfully put forward two suggestions regarding improving students’ physical fitness. First and foremost, **it would be highly beneficial if you could** organize more diverse sports activities on campus. This approach will effectively spark students’ interest in physical exercise. Moreover, **steps should also be taken to** provide awards or incentives to those who work out regularly. Specifically, beyond **ensuring the regularity of students’ daily exercise**, this measure will also significantly **set a positive example for others**, which is conducive to **cultivating a healthy and energetic campus atmosphere**. I sincerely hope that you will take these suggestions into consideration. Thank you for your time and attention. Yours sincerely, Li Ming

# 第二类人际情谊

Dear [称呼],  

I hope this email finds you in high spirits. I am writing to extend my **[heartfelt gratitude / sincere apology / warmest congratulations / cordial invitation]** to you for **[事由名词短语 / doing sth.]**.
- **【感谢/祝贺】过渡句：** Looking back on the past few days/months, what impresses me most is **[你的付出/你的努力]**. Specifically, **[细节 1：具体做了什么]**. Moreover, had it not been for **[你的帮助/你的坚持]**, **[细节 2：带来的好结果（用虚拟语气拔高句式）]**.



- **【道歉】过渡句：** Much to my regret, I am unable to **[原定计划]** as scheduled due to **[突发原因]**. Specifically, **[解释原因细节]**. To make up for this inconvenience, **[提出补救措施/改期方案]**.
    
- **【邀请】过渡句：** The **[活动名称]**, scheduled to be held at **[地点]** on **[时间]**, aims to **[活动目的]**. Given your profound expertise/keen interest in **[相关领域]**, we would be immensely honored if you could **[希望对方做的事，如 deliver a speech / join us]**.

Once again, please accept my **[sincere thanks / profound apologies / best wishes]**. **[如果是邀请/道歉需加一句：I would appreciate it if you could confirm your availability / accept my apology at your earliest convenience.]** Wish you all the best in your future endeavors.  
Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

# 第三类事务与申请


#### **第一段：寒暄 + 直奔意图（约 25 词）**

- **【申请/求职/志愿者】**  
    Dear Sir or Madam, (不知道具体姓名时用这个，注意依然有逗号！知道名字用 Dear Professor Smith,)  
    I am writing this letter to formally apply for the position of **[职位/志愿者名称，如 student volunteer / teaching assistant]** advertised on **[渠道，如 the campus bulletin board]** yesterday.
    
- **【推荐人】**  
    Dear Sir or Madam,  
    It is my great pleasure to write this letter to strongly recommend **[被推荐人，如 my classmate, Zhang Wei]** for the position of **[申请的职位/项目]** in your esteemed **[organization / university]**.
    
- **【辞职（极少考，备一个句式防身）】**  
    I am writing to formally notify you of my decision to resign from my position as **[职位]**, effective from **[生效日期，如 next Friday]**.
    

#### **第二段：展示核心竞争力 —— 你的“血肉区”（3 句话，约 65 词）**

- **逻辑：** 总体匹配度过渡句 + 能力 1 与能力 2（用 Beyond 结构） + 过去的相关经验/品格（用定语从句拔高）。
    
- **骨架（以申请信 I 为例，若写推荐信把 I 换成 He/She 即可）：**
    
    1. **总体匹配（主语从句/原因状语）：** Having carefully reviewed the requirements, I am convinced that my qualifications and experience make me a competitive candidate. (我确信我的资质和经验使我成为一名有竞争力的候选人。)

    2. **硬实力展示（直接用我们刚学的 Beyond 淘汰 not only！）：** **Beyond possessing a solid command of** **[硬技能 1，如 spoken English / computer science]**, I **also demonstrate exceptional ability in** **[硬技能 2/软实力，如 interpersonal communication / organizing campus activities]**.
        
    3. **经验加持（过去经历 + which 定语从句）：** Moreover, I once served as **[过去的经历，如 a volunteer in an international conference]** last year, **which** equips me with practical experience to handle unexpected challenges efficiently.


#### **第三段：表态 + 期待面试/回复（约 25 词）**

- **模板句：**  
    Should you grant me the opportunity for an interview, I would be more than delighted to discuss my qualifications in greater detail. Thank you for your time and consideration.  
    (右下角落款)  
    Yours sincerely, (如果开头是 Dear Sir or Madam, 严谨起见落款可用 **Yours faithfully,**，但考研里写 Yours sincerely 也不扣分)  
    Li Ming

  
codeText

```
Dear Sir or Madam,

    I am writing this letter to formally apply for the position of student volunteer for the International Cultural Exchange Week advertised on the campus bulletin board yesterday.

    Having carefully reviewed the requirements, I am convinced that my qualifications and experience make me a competitive candidate. Beyond possessing a solid command of spoken English, I also demonstrate exceptional ability in cross-cultural communication and introducing traditional Chinese culture. Moreover, I once served as a tour guide for foreign exchange students last year, which equips me with practical experience to handle unexpected challenges efficiently.

    Should you grant me the opportunity for an interview, I would be more than delighted to discuss my qualifications in greater detail. Thank you for your time and consideration.

                                                     Yours sincerely,
                                                              Li Ming
```
