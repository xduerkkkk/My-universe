### 一、 【第 1 类：诉求与解决类】（建议信 / 投诉信 / 回复朋友咨询）

#### 📌 母版 1A：朋友/对私版（以 2024 真题“给朋友 Paul 支招如何准备中国古代科学家口头报告”为例）
> **📖 【第一步：先背中文剧本（30 秒锁死逻辑）】**
> 1. **第一段：** 很高兴收到你的邮件询问关于【中国古代科学家口头报告】的事。我写信是为了分享两条实用的建议供你参考。
> 2. **第二段（2 条建议合为一段）：** 首先，你不妨去图书馆查阅相关资料或向教授请教。这一方法能有效帮你积累扎实的历史素材。此外，你还应当提前做好充分准备并反复练习。具体而言，除了能纠正发音和润色讲稿外，这一举措还能极大缓解心理压力并建立自信，这有助于你呈现出一场精彩的报告。
> 3. **第三段：** 我真诚希望你会觉得这些建议有用。祝你在未来的努力中一切顺利！

> **✍️ 【第二步：看着上面的中文，回译出英文母版】**
> Dear Paul,
> 　　**I am delighted to receive your email inquiring about** <u>the oral report on ancient Chinese scientists</u>. **I am writing to share two practical suggestions for your reference.**
> 　　**First and foremost, it is advisable for you to** <u>consult relevant materials in the library or seek professional guidance from professors</u>. **This approach will effectively** <u>help you accumulate solid historical facts</u>. **Moreover, you may also consider** <u>making full preparations and practicing repeatedly in advance</u>. **Specifically, beyond** <u>correcting your pronunciation and polishing your draft</u>, **this measure will also significantly** <u>alleviate mental pressure and build up your confidence</u>, **which is conducive to** <u>delivering an impressive presentation</u>.
> 　　**I sincerely hope that you will find these suggestions helpful. Wish you all the best in your future endeavors.**
> <div align="right">Yours sincerely,<br>Li Ming</div>
