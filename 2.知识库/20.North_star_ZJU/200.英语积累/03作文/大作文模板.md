# 🌟 【母版 1：个人成长与品质类】（通杀：坚持、读书、自律、选择、迎难而上）

- **母题背景：** 两个男生面对厚厚一摞书，一个畏难抱怨读不完，一个微笑每天坚持读一点。
    

**【第一段：双图对比描述（68 词）】**  
**Vividly depicted in the two cartoons is a sharp contrast between two different attitudes toward** reading. **In the first picture, faced with** a thick stack of books, a boy is frowning in anxiety, mumbling that it is too difficult to finish reading them; **whereas in the second one,** another boy with a confident smile is reading a book attentively, saying that taking one step at a time leads to great progress. **Simple as the drawing appears, the symbolic meaning behind it is profound: it highlights the vital importance of** down-to-earth action and perseverance.

**【第二段：正反对比深度论证（90 词）】**  
**Actually, there is a growing consensus that** perseverance and self-discipline **serve as the cornerstone of** academic and personal fulfillment. **When confronted with** daunting challenges in our study or life, shrinking back in anxiety or giving up halfway **will only trap us in** endless stagnation and self-doubt. **Conversely, by** breaking an overwhelming goal into manageable daily steps, **beyond** alleviating mental pressure, **we can also** accumulate profound knowledge steadily and unlock our full potential. **Just as an old saying goes,** “Constant dropping wears away a stone.”

**【第三段：青年呼吁与升华（42 词）】**  
**Taking all these factors into consideration, it is imperative that we university students should** abandon the mentality of procrastination and translate our lofty ideals into concrete actions. **Only in this way can we** overcome unexpected obstacles and stride forward toward a promising future.

>     
> 3. 综上所述，我们大学生迫切需要摒弃拖延心态，将远大理想转化为具体行动。唯有如此，我们才能克服意想不到的障碍，朝着光明的未来稳步迈进。

# 🌟 【母版 2：社会现象与文化弘扬类】（通杀：传统文化、全民健身、环保、社会美德）

- **母题背景（结合2023赛龙舟/2024公园健身房真题）：** 一群市民正兴高采烈地参加传统民俗活动（或全民健身），旁边的字幕写着“民俗回归/健康生活真好”。
    

**【第一段：单图群像描述（62词）】**  
**Vividly depicted in the cartoon is a heartwarming and inspiring scene:** a large crowd of citizens are enthusiastically participating in a traditional dragon boat race. **Specifically, with bright smiles on their faces,** spectators on the bank are cheering excitedly, with the caption reading: “Traditional customs are booming again!” **Simple as the drawing appears, the symbolic meaning behind it is profound: it highlights the vital importance of** inheriting and promoting fine traditional Chinese culture.

**【第二段：原因分析 + 双重意义论证（88词）】**  
**Actually, there is a growing consensus that** cultural inheritance **serves as the cornerstone of** national identity and social harmony. **Several primary factors account for this encouraging phenomenon.** **For one thing,** with the rapid improvement of living standards, people are increasingly pursuing spiritual enrichment rather than merely material wealth. **For another, beyond** enriching citizens' leisure life and strengthening social bonds, embracing time-honored traditions **can also** foster a strong sense of cultural confidence among the younger generation.

**【第三段：全社会呼吁与升华（40词）】**  
**Taking all these factors into consideration, it is imperative that concrete measures should be taken to** preserve our cultural heritage and integrate it into modern life. **Only in this way can we** keep our traditions alive and build a more vibrant and harmonious society.

> **📖 【母版2·看中文复述自测卡】**
> 
> 1. 漫画中生动描绘了一幅温馨而鼓舞人心的场景：大批市民正热情高涨地参加传统赛龙舟活动。具体而言，岸上的观众面带灿烂微笑、兴奋欢呼，字幕写着：“传统民俗又火了！”尽管图画看似简单，其背后的象征意义却很深远：它凸显了传承与弘扬中华优秀传统文化的至关重要性。
>     
> 2. 事实上，人们日益达成共识：文化传承是民族认同与社会和谐的基石。有几个主要因素可以解释这一令人鼓舞的现象。一方面，随着生活水平的快速提高，人们日益追求精神富足而非仅仅是物质财富。另一方面，除了能丰富市民的业余生活、增进社会纽带外，拥抱历史悠久的传统还能在年轻一代中培养强烈的文化自信。(注：若考“全民运动/环保”，只需把“精神富足/文化自信”换为 physical well-being / sustainable development 即可！)

# 🌟 【母版 3：科技与双刃剑反思类】（通杀：手机依赖、AI冲击、网络社交、碎片化阅读）

- **母题背景（经典真题母题）：** 一群年轻人坐在一起聚餐（或在自习室学习），但每个人都低头盯着智能手机/过度依赖AI，彼此毫无交流（或失去了独立思考）。
    

**【第一段：讽刺单图描述（64词）】**  
**Vividly depicted in the cartoon is a thought-provoking and ironic scene:** several young people are sitting together at a dinner table, yet everyone is staring intently at their smartphones. **Specifically,** instead of communicating with each other face to face, they are completely immersed in the虚拟 (virtual) world, leaving the delicious food untouched. **Simple as the drawing appears, the symbolic meaning behind it is profound: it warns us against** excessive reliance on modern digital technology.

**【第二段：让步转折（双刃剑）论证（86词）】**  
**Granted, nobody can deny that** cutting-edge technologies like smartphones and AI have brought unprecedented convenience to our daily communication and learning. **However, if we allow ourselves to be enslaved by** digital screens, **the consequences can be detrimental.** **Beyond** eroding our capacity for independent and deep thinking, indulging in the virtual world **will also** alienate us from our family and friends in reality, trapping us in spiritual isolation. **After all, technology is meant to be a useful tool rather than our master.**

**【第三段：理性呼吁与升华（42词）】**  
**Taking all these factors into consideration, it is imperative that we should** strike a healthy balance between the virtual world and real life, and utilize modern technology in a rational manner. **Only in this way can we** truly enjoy the dividends of technological progress without losing ourselves.

>
