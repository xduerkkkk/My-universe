**太对了！** 写大作文第二段时，**不要自己造新词**，拿【母版 2 社会现象类】的骨架，把下面这几个刚需词填进去就行：

- **本篇只需新记 4 个主题词（其余全用你背过的模板词！）：**
    
    1. continuing education（继续教育）/ lifelong learning（终身学习）
        
    2. current jobs（现有工作）
        
    3. diplomas or certificates（文凭或证书）
        
    4. fierce competition in the job market（职场激烈竞争）
        
- **看我怎么用“母版 2 + 你的思路”拼出 06 的核心血肉（不用背全文，看懂拼接逻辑即可）：**
    
    - **第一段（图表补丁版）：**  
        **Vividly illustrated in the bar chart are** the varied purposes of working adults pursuing continuing education in 2025. **Specifically,** the majority prioritize improving skills for their current jobs (65%) and learning knowledge in new fields (48%), followed by developing personal interests (37%). **By contrast, far fewer people (26%) aim to** merely obtain diplomas or certificates. **Simple as the statistics appear, the message behind them is profound:** modern workers increasingly value **practical competence** over mere paper qualifications.  
        (注：多选题比例之和超 100%，用 prioritize... followed by... By contrast, far fewer... 完美规避了计算总和的麻烦，而且一笔带过所有数据！)
        
    - **第二段（完全复用你的母版 2 骨架，只换 2 处血肉！）：**  
        **Actually, there is a growing consensus that** lifelong learning **serves as the cornerstone of** career development and personal fulfillment (偷母版 1！). **Several primary factors account for this encouraging phenomenon. For one thing,** with the rapid upgrading of technology and fierce competition in the job market, employees have to update their skills constantly to stay competitive (这就是你要写的“职业市场需求”！). **For another, beyond** boosting professional competence, acquiring new knowledge and developing personal interests **can also** bring spiritual enrichment (偷母版 2！) and unlock their full potential (偷母版 1！).
