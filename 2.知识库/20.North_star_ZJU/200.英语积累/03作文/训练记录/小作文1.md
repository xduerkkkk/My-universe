1. 三个绝对不能犯的硬伤（直接扣分点）
*   ❌ `To make up for this convenience`
    *   **致命反义词！** `convenience` 是“方便”，这里明明是“为了弥补这个**不便**”，必须用 **`inconvenience`**！写反了意思直接颠倒。另外后面又写了一遍 `ensure your convenience`，用词重复。
*   ❌ `a online pick-up service`
    *   **基础冠词错误！** `online` 元音音素开头，必须用 **`an`** online pick-up service。
*   ❌ `I would be appreciate if you could...`
    *   **背串模板了！** 你自己 2C 模板里写的是 `I would appreciate it if...`（`appreciate` 是动词，不能前面加 `be`！）。要么用 **`I would appreciate it if...`**，要么用 `I would be grateful if...`。**这个坑一定要用红笔圈出来！**

2. 模板生搬硬套导致的“逻辑违和”（冲 8 分必改）
*   ⚠️ **第一段细节缺失：** 题目明确说了 `this Saturday` 和 `at the airport`，你只写了 `pick you up as scheduled`。加上 `pick you up at the airport this Saturday as scheduled`，审题扣合度瞬间拉满。
*   ⚠️ `for upcoming International Cultural Exchange Week`
    *   缺定冠词 **`the`**！特指即将到来的这一届，必须是 `for the upcoming...`（你原模板里也有 `the`）。
*   ⚠️ `conflict with our appointment`
    *   `appointment` 多指约见医生、教授、商务会面。朋友来机场接机，用 **`your arrival`**（你的抵达）或 **`our original plan`** 更地道。
*   ⚠️ `I have called a... whose car will come to the airport punctually. By then, I will remind them again...`
    *   **中式表达 + 逻辑微瑕：** “叫网约车”不用 `call`（那是打电话），用 **`booked / arranged`**（预订/安排）；`whose car will come...` 略显生硬，改为 **`whose driver will meet you at the airport on time`**；另外 `By then`（到那时候再提醒）来不及了，应该在**他出发前**提醒，改为 `Before Saturday, I will confirm with the driver again to ensure a smooth trip.`
*   ⚠️ `confirm your availability`（**你的直觉延伸**）
    *   原模板 2C 是“推迟和教授的见面时间”，所以让他“确认什么时候有空（availability）”。但 James 的**航班时间周六是固定的**，不存在“他什么时候有空”的问题！这里应该让他确认**“这个接机方案行不行 / 把航班号发给你”**。


*   **分段不统一：** 第 1、2、4 段都缩进了，唯独 `To make up for this convenience...` 顶格写了，而且上一行 `appointment` 挤在纸边缘还涂改了。考场上按 2C 母版直接写成**三段式**（第 2 段包含原因+解决方案，不拆开），既整齐又不容易忘缩进。
*   **涂改：** 全篇有 4~5 处涂改（`fix`, `the`, `up`, `you`）。考场要求“零涂改”或极少涂改，写之前脑子里先过一遍完整从句再落笔。

# 中国传统文化周（介绍+建议）
假设你是李明，你的外国朋友 Paul 下周要来你们学校参加“中国传统文化周（Traditional Chinese Culture Week）”，他发邮件向你咨询活动的具体安排，并希望你给他一些参加活动的建议。请给他回信。


Dear Paul,  
**I am delighted to receive your email inquiring about** the specific arrangements of the upcoming Traditional Chinese Culture Week. **I am writing to** outline the relevant details and **share some practical suggestions for your reference.**  
**The event, scheduled to be held at the Campus Culture Square next week, aims to spark keen interest in inheriting and promoting fine traditional Chinese culture. Specifically, it features a variety of engaging activities**, such as classic poem recitation and a cultural quiz. **Moreover, you may also consider making full preparations in advance** by watching some relevant documentaries. **Beyond helping you accumulate solid** background knowledge, **this measure will also significantly** deepen your understanding, **which is conducive to** enjoying the event to the fullest.  
**I sincerely hope that you will find** this information and **these suggestions helpful. I warmly anticipate your active participation.**  
**Yours sincerely,**  
**Li Ming**
