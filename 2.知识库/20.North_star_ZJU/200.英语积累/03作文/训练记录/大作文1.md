---

 一、第一段怎么写？（主语优化 + 5 组数据秒杀法）

1. 你的第一句怎么修？
* **你写的：** `the change in commuting method choice of resident`
* **小毛病：** `method choice` 名词堆砌略显中式；`resident` 是可数名词，不能光着身子裸奔（要加复数 `s`）。
* **降维地道版：** **`the change in residents' daily commuting choices`**（居民日常出行选择的变化）。短小精悍，0 语法风险！

5 组数据那么多，怎么套【插件 A】？（牢记：**抓大放小，同类合并**！）
图里有 5 项，**千万别一项项报流水账**！一眼看穿核心反差：
* **大降组：** 公共汽车（52% $\rightarrow$ 27%），私家车略降；
* **暴涨组：** 网约车（约 9% $\rightarrow$ 25%）和共享单车（约 7% $\rightarrow$ 24%）——**巧了！俩人起点都在 10% 以下，终点都在 25% 左右！合并一句话带过！**

直接套我们刚才统一的【3 句骨架 + 插件 A】：
> **Vividly illustrated in the chart is the change in** residents' daily commuting choices from 2015 to 2021. **Specifically, while** taking buses **declined sharply from** about 52% **in** 2015 **to** 27% **in** 2021, **both** online ride-hailing **and** shared bicycles **witnessed a dramatic increase from** under 10% **to** nearly 25% each. **Simple as the chart appears, the message behind it is profound: it highlights** the trend toward smarter and greener urban commuting.

*(你看！用 `from under 10% to nearly 25% each` 一句话吞掉 4 个数据，完全不用硬写精确到小数点的数字，又准又高级！)*

---

 二、第二段：非黑即白的中性现象，怎么套 `growing consensus`？

你问得太好了！像“居民出行变化 / 便利店数量增长 / 冰激凌线上线下消费”这类**中性偏正向的社会发展趋势**，确实不是“好 vs 坏”的二元对立，那怎么填【母版 2】的第一句呢？

**秘诀：把主题提炼成“中性偏正向的宏观价值”（如：便捷高效的出行 / 多元化的生活方式 / 智慧绿色交通），直接塞进 `cornerstone` 句型，丝毫不违和！**

你看我把**你刚才自己想出的两个完美理由**，原封不动塞进你的【母版 2】，是不是丝滑得像原生范文：

> **Actually, there is a growing consensus that** <u>efficient and green transportation</u> **serves as the cornerstone of** <u>modern urban life</u>. **Several primary factors account for this encouraging phenomenon.**
> * **For one thing,** *(塞入你的理由 1：网络普及+便利)* <u>with the rapid development of mobile internet, emerging services like online ride-hailing and shared bikes provide unprecedented convenience and flexibility for daily commuters</u>.
> * **For another,** *(塞入你的理由 2：低碳环保意识 + 复用母版 `beyond... can also...`)* **beyond** <u>alleviating traffic congestion and reducing carbon emissions</u>, <u>choosing green travel modes like shared bicycles</u> **can also** <u>promote a healthier lifestyle as citizens' environmental awareness improves</u>.

1. **第二段首句：**
    
    - 你的思路：新能源是 social economic 和 environment protection 的 cornerstone？
        
    - **纠正搭配：** economic 是形容词，protection 是名词，所以要用 environmental protection（环境保护）。实在怕拼错 environmental，直接用 **green development**（绿色发展）或 **sustainable development**（可持续发展）！
        
    - **成品：** Actually, there is a growing consensus that the development of new energy vehicles serves as the cornerstone of economic growth and environmental protection.
        
2. **For one thing（你的原意：随着技术发展，人们追求更环保的科技）：**
    
    - 你的卡点：more environmental 的科技不会说。

    - **成品：** For one thing, with the advancement of technology and growing environmental awareness, consumers increasingly prefer green products.（完全用初中词汇，零失误！）
        
3. **For another（你的原意：新能源的设施给这些车主带来更多方便）：**
    
    - 你的卡点：make more convenience for 这些车主（中式语法！没有 make convenience 这个搭配）。
        
    - **固定搭配记死：** “给……带来极大便利” = **bring great convenience to...**（或用形容词 make daily travel more convenient for...）；“配套设施” = **supporting facilities**（或直接写 charging facilities 充电设施）；“车主” = **car owners** 或 **drivers**！
        
    - **成品：** For another, beyond reducing air pollution, improving supporting facilities has brought great convenience to car owners.
