### 🔍 逐段精批与“捉虫”诊断

#### 【第一段：描图与寓意】
> **你的原文：** Vividly depicted in the two **cartoon** is a sharp contrast between two different attitudes toward academic study in **digital** age. In the first picture, faced with a writing task, a boy is relying on AI tools to generate his **paper mumbling** that **it's** so easy **to be done.** **Whereas** in the second one, another boy is ~~consulting~~ consulting books in **library** and thinking independently, saying that **one step** at a time leads to great progress. Simple as the drawing appears, the symbolic meaning behind it is profound: it **warn** us against excessive reliance on modern technology, and highlights the vital importance of independent thinking.

*   **🐛 捉虫与修正（重点抓手滑！）：**
    1. **单复数与冠词漏写（连环手滑）：**
       * `two cartoon` $\rightarrow$ **`two cartoons`**（前面有 two，必须加 s！）。
       * `in digital age` $\rightarrow$ **`in the digital age`**（特指数字时代，加 the）。
       * `in library` $\rightarrow$ **`in the library`**（小作文【1A】原词就是 *in the library*，别吃掉冠词！）。
    2. **分词作伴随状语漏逗号：** `generate his paper, mumbling that...`（在 *paper* 后面加个逗号，层次更清晰）。
    3. **口语缩写与冗余：** `it's so easy to be done.` $\rightarrow$ **`it is so easy.`**（大作文严禁写 *it's*！而且 *easy to do* 用主动表被动，不要写 *to be done*，直接写 *it is so easy* 最安全）。
    4. **`Whereas` 破句警告：** *Whereas* 是连词（虽然/然而），引导从句，**前面最好用分号 `;` 或逗号 `,` 连接上一句**（看你母版 1，写的是 `; whereas in the second one...`）。如果非要句号开头，建议换成副词 **`Conversely,`**。
    5. **主语缺失（语法硬伤）：** `saying that one step at a time leads to...` $\rightarrow$ **`saying that taking one step at a time leads to...`**。*one step at a time* 只是一个名词短语/状语，直接作主语稍显生硬，加上动名词 **`taking`**（母版 1 原词）才地道！
    6. **主谓一致红牌：** `it warn us` $\rightarrow$ **`it warns us`**！后面你都知道写 *and highlights*（加了 s），前面的 *warn* 怎么能忘加 s 呢！

#### 【第二段：正反论证】
> **你的原文：** Granted, nobody can deny that AI technologies have brought unprecedented convenience to our learning. However, if we allow ourselves to be ~~ensalved~~ enslaved by AI tools, the consequence will be detrimental. Beyond eroding our capacity for independent and deep thinking, relying on AI will also trap us in laziness, **preventing us from endeavour**. Conversely, by consulting relevant materials and practicing repeatedly, we can accumulate profound knowledge and unlock our full potential step by step. **Just an** old saying goes, "Constant dropping wears away a stone."

*   **🐛 捉虫与修正：**
    1. **自己造词翻车：`preventing us from endeavour`**
       * 你想表达“阻止我们努力”，但 *endeavour* 作名词时通常指“一番具体的尝试/事业”（可数）或“尽力”，直接写 *prevent us from endeavour* 非常中式且不地道！
       * **怎么改？听老师的：不要自己临场编词！直接抄母版 1 的原词！** 你前面写了 *trap us in laziness*（或母版 1 的 *endless stagnation*），后面根本不用再加分词了；如果非要加，抄母版 3 的 **`alienating us from true academic fulfillment`**（使我们远离真正的学术成就），或者改成 **`hindering our academic progress`**（阻碍我们的学业进步）。
    1. **名言漏词（极其可惜的手滑）：** `Just an old saying goes` $\rightarrow$ **`Just as an old saying goes`**！你把最关键的连词 **`as`** 给漏掉了！没了 *as*，这句话在语法上就成了没有连词的双谓语病句！

#### 【第三段：总结建议 —— ⚠️ 虚拟语气语法红牌！】
> **你的原文：** Taking all these factors into consideration, it is imperative that we university students **need** perseverance and self-discipline rather than immersing ourselves in AI tools. Only in this way can we **enhance the depth of thought**.

*   **🐛 捉虫与修正（必看！）：**
    1. **`it is imperative that...` 的虚拟语气：**
       * 这是你母版 1、2、3 共同的压轴句型！`It is imperative that sb. (should) do sth.` 里面必须用 **`should + 动词原形`**（或者省略 should 直接用动词原形）。
       * 你写成了 `should` 删掉、換成了 `need perseverance`（需要毅力），不仅破坏了虚拟语气的规整感，而且把母版里动作感极强的动词短语改成了静态状态。
       * **怎么改？锁死母版原词：** `...it is imperative that we university students should cultivate perseverance and self-discipline...`（我们必须**培养**毅力和自律），或者直接抄母版 3：**`...should utilize modern technology in a rational manner`**！
    1. **结尾句不够响亮：** `enhance the depth of thought`（提升思想深度）稍微有点像中文硬翻（一般说 *deepen our thoughts*）。建议最后一句**100%锁死母版 1 或母版 3 的原句**，千万别自己改！因为阅卷老师看最后一句最看重节奏感：
       * 要么抄母版 1：`Only in this way can we overcome unexpected obstacles and stride forward toward a promising future.`
       * 要么抄母版 3：`Only in this way can we truly enjoy the dividends of technological progress without losing ourselves.`（唯有如此，我们才能真正享受科技进步的红利而不迷失自我——多契合 AI 主题啊！）
