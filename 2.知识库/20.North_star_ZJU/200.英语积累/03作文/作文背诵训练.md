# 作文背诵训练

> 素材来源：[[小作文背诵]]（7篇小作文母版）、[[大作文模板]]（3篇大作文母版）。小作文以小作文母版为准，大作文以大作文母版为准；母版修改后再同步对应内容。
> 本页实际有 **10类骨架、43个固定句编号提示、84个短语编号提示**。一个固定句提示可能包含几种写法，不等于只有43个英文句子。本文的“2C道歉信”对应小作文母版中的“3C道歉信”，只是编号不同。

## 怎么用

**本页只作统一背诵材料，不再放逐日训练计划。** 按位置背固定句，按抽屉背短语；不用每天按专题跨组找编号。七天安排见[[0.核心/00.规划(Plan)/2.当前项目/当前冲刺规划#作文｜7天集中背诵|当前冲刺规划]]。

- **先遮答案回忆，再核对。** 已经看过多次，不必先从头通读一遍；看中文，尝试说出英文，卡住才展开答案。
- **固定句**：整组回忆；口头背为主，想不出、语法不稳或拼写拿不准的句子落笔核对。用途不同的写法要分清，例如对公／对私、感谢／道歉／邀请；不是只记斜杠前一版。
- **短语**：整抽屉回忆，重点检查介词、动词形式和拼写；拿不准就写。不要只看英文觉得眼熟。
- **骨架**：只看标题，说出每段写什么，不逐字背中文。
- **卡点**：沿用原有 `@`、`！` 等标记，想不出或背错可在原条目旁加 `❌`；回头优先检查这些地方，不另建打卡表。刚看完立即背对，不等于隔天仍能记住。

## 一、骨架链（只看标题，说出每段槽位）

> [!question]- 1A 建议信·对私（给朋友）
> - P1：收到来信（询问 X）→ 写信分享两条建议
> - P2：建议 1 做法 → 建议 1 好处 → 建议 2 做法 → Beyond 好处 A, also 好处 B, which 长远效果
> - P3：希望有用 → 祝愿


> [!question]- 1B 建议信·对公（给校长/部门）
> - P1：引起关注 + 就 X 提两条建议
> - P2：同 1A，句式换成对公版（it would be highly beneficial if you could / steps should also be taken to）
> - P3：希望采纳 → 感谢时间


> [!question]- 2A 邀请信
> - P1：寒暄 → 邀请参加 X
> - P2：活动时间地点 + 目的 → 鉴于你的专长，荣幸请你做 Y → Beyond A, also B, which 有益且有启发
> - P3：请确认时间 → 祝愿


> [!question]- 2B 感谢信
> - P1：寒暄 → 感谢你在 X 中的支持
> - P2：回顾：最打动我的是你的鼓励 → 具体：正是你做了… → 要不是你的指导，我不可能…（虚拟语气）
> - P3：再次感谢 → 祝愿


> [!question]- 2C 道歉信
> - P1：寒暄 → 道歉：无法按计划做 X
> - P2：遗憾：因突发事务调整日程 → 具体原因，与约定冲突 → 补救：能否改到… → 届时充分准备
> - P3：再次道歉 → 请确认时间 → 祝愿



> [!question]- 3 申请信
> - P1：正式申请 X 职位（某渠道发布）
> - P2：看完要求，确信自己有竞争力 → Beyond 技能 1, also 能力 2 → 曾任…, which 带来处理突发挑战的经验
> - P3：若给面试机会，乐意详谈 → 感谢
> - 推荐信：P1 换成 It is my great pleasure to write this letter to strongly recommend…，P2 把 I 换成 He/She



> [!question]- 4 Notice 通知
> - 格式：居中 NOTICE + 右上日期，右下落款写部门
> - P1：本通知旨在为[群体]宣布 X → 细节如下
> - P2：时间地点 + 目的 → 包含系列活动；Beyond A, also B → 请记住：参与者须…
> - P3：详情联系邮箱 → 期待参与




> [!question]- 大 1 个人品质（双图对比）
> - P1：双图对比引出 → 图 1 消极 whereas 图 2 积极 → 寓意：凸显 X 的重要性
> - P2：共识：X 是 Y 的基石 → 反面：面对挑战退缩只会陷入… → 正面：Conversely, by…, beyond A, also B → 谚语
> - P3：综上，大学生必须… → Only in this way can we…

> [!question]- 大 2 社会文化（单图群像）
> - P1：单图场景引出 → 具体：面带笑容…，字幕 → 寓意
> - P2：共识：X 是 Y 的基石 → 几个原因 → For one thing 生活水平提高、追求精神 → For another, beyond A, also B
> - P3：综上，应采取措施… → Only in this way can we…

> [!question]- 大 3 科技双刃剑（讽刺单图）
> - P1：讽刺场景 → 具体：不面对面交流而沉浸虚拟世界 → 寓意：警醒过度依赖
> - P2：诚然带来便利 → 然而被奴役，后果有害 → Beyond 侵蚀思考, also 疏远亲友 → 毕竟是工具而非主人
> - P3：综上，平衡虚实、理性使用 → Only in this way can we…享受红利

## 二、钢筋（看中文，逐字写出英文）

`[ ]` 是需要填具体内容的位置。背诵时可用原母版中的内容补全；以后写作时按题意改，不能随便塞一个短语。

### A 组：小作文开头

1. 希望这封邮件找到您时，您精神饱满。
2. 我写信是为了向您表达由衷的感激 / 诚挚的歉意 / 诚挚的邀请（三种都要会，注意介词）!
3. 很高兴收到你询问 [ ] 的邮件。我写信是为了分享两条实用的建议供你参考。
4. 我写这封信是为了引起您对一个问题的关注，并就 [ ] 恭敬地提出两条建议。!
5. 我写信正式申请昨天在 [校园布告栏] 上发布的 [ ] 一职。!
6. 本通知旨在为 [群体] 宣布 [ ]。相关细节概述如下。!


> [!success]- 答案
> 1. I hope this email finds you in high spirits.
> 2. I am writing to extend my heartfelt gratitude to you **for** [ ]. / I am writing to extend my sincere apology to you **for being unable to** [ ] as scheduled. / I am writing to extend my cordial invitation to you **to attend** [ ].
> 3. I am delighted to receive your email inquiring about [ ]. I am writing to share two practical suggestions for your reference.
> 4. I am writing this letter to bring an issue to your attention and respectfully put forward two suggestions regarding [ ].
> 5. I am writing this letter to formally apply for the position of [ ] advertised on [the campus bulletin board] yesterday.
> 6. This notice is issued to [announce the upcoming …] for [all international students]. The relevant details concerning this matter are outlined as follows.

### B 组：小作文主体

1. 首先，你不妨…（对私）/ 如果您能…将大有裨益（对公）
2. 这一方法将有效地…
3. 此外，你也可以考虑…（对私）/ 还应采取措施…（对公）
4. **【万能展开句】** 具体而言，除了 A 之外，这一举措还将极大地 B，这有助于 C。
5. 该活动定于 [时间] 在 [地点] 举行，旨在…
6. 鉴于您在 [ ] 方面的深厚造诣，如果您能…，我们将深感荣幸。！
7. 回顾过去的一个月，最令我印象深刻的是…
8. 具体而言，正是您…!!
9. 此外，要不是您的 [ ]，我绝不可能…!!!
10. 令我深感遗憾的是，由于 [ ]，我不得不调整日程。
11. ……，这恰好与我们的约定冲突。!!
12. 为了弥补这一不便，不知我们能否把见面改到 [ ]。!!
13. 在仔细阅读要求后，我确信我的资质和经验使我成为一名有竞争力的候选人。!!
14. 除了扎实掌握 [ ] 外，我在 [ ] 方面也展现出卓越的能力。!!!
15. 此外，我去年曾担任 [ ]，这使我具备了高效处理突发挑战的实践经验。!!!
16. 最后，请记住所有参与者都须…

> [!success]- 答案
> 1. First and foremost, it is advisable for you to [do]. / First and foremost, it would be highly beneficial if you could [do].
> 2. This approach will effectively [do].
> 3. Moreover, you may also consider [doing]. / Moreover, steps should also be taken to [do].
> 4. Specifically, beyond [doing A], this measure will also significantly [do B], which is conducive to [名词 / doing C].
> 5. The [event], scheduled to be held at [地点] [时间], aims to [do].
> 6. Given your profound expertise in [ ], we would be immensely honored if you could [do].
> 7. Looking back on the past month, what impresses me most is [名词].
> 8. Specifically, it was you who [did].
> 9. Moreover, had it not been for [ ], I could never have [done].
> 10. Much to my regret, I have to adjust my schedule due to [an unexpected urgent matter].
> 11. …, which happens to conflict with our appointment.
> 12. To make up for this inconvenience, I wonder if we could reschedule our meeting for [next Monday afternoon].
> 13. Having carefully reviewed the requirements, I am convinced that my qualifications and experience make me a competitive candidate.
> 14. Beyond possessing a solid command of [ ], I also demonstrate exceptional ability in [ ].
> 15. Moreover, I once served as [ ] last year, which equips me with practical experience to handle unexpected challenges efficiently.
> 16. Finally, please keep in mind that all participants are expected to [do].

> [!warning] 易错点
> - beyond 是介词，后面接 **doing / 名词**，不接动词原形
> - consider 后接 **doing**；it is advisable for you to / steps should be taken to 后接**动词原形**
> - had it not been for 后接**名词**，主句用 could never have **done**
> - I wonder if… 是陈述句，句末用句号

7. 如需更多详情或报名流程，请随时通过 [ ] 联系我们。我们热切期盼您的积极参与。！

> [!success]- 答案
> 1. I sincerely hope that you will find these suggestions helpful. / I sincerely hope that you will take these suggestions into consideration.
> 2. Thank you for your time and consideration.
> 3. I would appreciate it if you could confirm your availability at your earliest convenience.
> 4. Once again, please accept my sincere thanks. / Once again, please accept my profound apologies.
> 5. I wish you all the best in your future endeavors.（母版里写的是 Wish you…，开头补上 I 更规范）
> 6. Should you grant me the opportunity for an interview, I would be more than delighted to discuss my qualifications in greater detail.
> 7. For further details or application procedures, please do not hesitate to contact us at [ ]. We warmly anticipate your active participation.

### E 组：大作文第二段

1. 事实上，人们日益达成共识：[ ] 是 [ ] 的基石。
2. 当面对 [ ] 时，[消极做法] 只会让我们陷入 [ ]。

> 1. Actually, there is a growing consensus that [ ] serves as the cornerstone of [ ].
> 2. When confronted with [ ], [doing] will only trap us in [ ].
> 3. Conversely, by [doing], beyond [doing A], we can also [do B].
> 4. Just as an old saying goes, "Constant dropping wears away a stone." / "Rome was not built in a day."
> 5. Several primary factors account for this encouraging phenomenon. For one thing, [ ]. For another, beyond [doing A], [主语] can also [do B].
> 6. Granted, nobody can deny that [ ] have brought unprecedented convenience to [ ]. However, if we allow ourselves to be enslaved by [ ], the consequences can be detrimental.
> 7. After all, technology is meant to be a useful tool rather than our master.
