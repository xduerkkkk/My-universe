
| 归并类别          | 涵盖的具体题型              | 底层逻辑                                                  |
| :------------ | :------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. 诉求与解决类** | **建议信、投诉信、咨询信**      | 说明问题/现状 $\to$ 提出 2~3 点具体建议或要求 $\to$ 期待改进或答复           |
| **2. 人际与情谊类** | **感谢信、道歉信、邀请信、祝贺信**  | 表达情绪（谢/歉/喜/邀） $\to$ 阐述细节/原因 $\to$ 再次表达心意与美好祝愿         |
| **3. 事务与申请类** | **申请/求职信、辞职信、推荐信**   | 说明申请/辞职/推荐意图 $\to$ 列举能力、资质或客观理由 $\to$ 期待面试/批准         |
| **4. 公开告示类**  | **Notice（通知）、招募、倡议** | 格式是通知排版，内容为：活动背景/目的 $\to$ 时间/地点/要求/流程 $\to$ 呼吁参与/联系方式 |

# 第一类诉求建议 

#### 小类 1：严肃/对公版（写给机构、校长、部门、报社）

> **Dear [称谓，如：President / Sir or Madam / the Editor],**
> 
> 　　I am writing this letter to **[写信目的，如：bring an urgent issue to your attention / express my concerns about ...]**, and respectfully put forward several constructive suggestions regarding **[主题词/动名词短语，如：improving campus facilities]**.
> 
> 　　To begin with, it would be highly beneficial if **[建议一的做法，从句用 were/could do]**. This approach will effectively **[这样做带来的具体效果/目的]**. Furthermore, steps should be taken to **[建议二的做法，接动词原形]**. Specifically, **[进一步解释建议二的具体操作细节]**. Last but not least, I would also recommend that **[建议三，接从句 (should) do]**, which will definitely **[带来的长远好处]**.
> 
> 　　I sincerely hope that you will take these suggestions into consideration. Thank you for your time and kind attention.
> 
> Yours sincerely,  
> Li Ming

#### 小类 2：朋友/对私版（写给朋友、同学、新生）

> **Dear [朋友名字，如：Paul / Jack],**
> 
> 　　I am delighted to receive your email inquiring about **[对方咨询的事情，如：job-hunting in China / preparing your presentation]**. Understanding that you are **[对方目前的处境，如：looking for a suitable job / preparing for the oral report]**, I am writing to share a few practical suggestions for your reference.
> 
> 　　First and foremost, you may consider **[建议一，接动名词 doing]**. This is because **[原因/理由：这样做为什么重要]**. Additionally, it is advisable for you to **[建议二，接动词原形 do]**. Specifically, you could **[展开的具体操作做法]**. Finally, why not **[建议三，接动词原形 do]**? In this way, you will be able to **[最终收获或效果]**.
> 
> 　　I hope you find these recommendations helpful, and I am confident that **[祝愿/鼓励，如：you will achieve great success / give an impressive presentation]**. Please feel free to reach out if you need further help.
> 
> Yours sincerely, (或 Yours warmly,)  
> Li Ming
