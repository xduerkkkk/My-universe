### 句子 1

**你的译文：** But even more **excited**, it is the farthest sea area human had been to, for what they were seeing **is** the feature **exsiting** millions of years ago.

- **⚠️ 85+ 修正点：**
    
    1. **形容词误用：** excited 是“感到兴奋的（人）”，修饰这件事（it）应该用 exciting。或者原文用的是 important。
        
    2. **时态一致性：** 既然前半句用了 had been（过去完成时），后面的 is 最好改成 was 或者 were，保持时态统一度。
        
    3. **拼写错误：** exsiting 
        
        ```
        →→
        ```
        
         **existing**。
        
    4. **表达升级：** had been to 有点口语化，模仿原文建议用 had been able to reach。
        
- **参考范文：**  
    But even more **exciting**, it was the farthest sea area **that** humans had been able to reach, **for** what they were seeing were creatures **that existed** millions of years ago.

### 句子 2

**你的译文：** The **sustain** investment **for fund** is **vitrually** required for this new **projxt**, **which first put forward last year**, to maintain **the** reign as the dominant **stragery** in company.

- **⚠️ 85+ 修正点：**
    
    1. **词性错误：** sustain 是动词，形容词是 sustained（持续的）。
        
    2. **词义重复：** investment for fund 意思重复了，直接 The continuous investment 即可。

        ```

        ```

        
         **project**; stragery

### 句子 3

**你的译文：** The meteorologists working with satellite **instrument is closing on** center of typhoon, and **maight** report their **measure data** soon

- **⚠️ 85+ 修正点：**
    
    1. **主谓不一致（大忌！）：** 主语是 meteorologists (复数)，谓语动词必须用 **are**，不能用 is。这是扣分重灾区。
        
    2. **短语缺失：** 模仿原文是 closing **in** on（逼近），你漏了 in。
        
    3. **拼写错误：** maight 
        
        ```
        →→
        ```
        
         **may** 或 **might**。
        
    4. **搭配优化：** measure data 
        
        ```
        →→
        ```
        
         measurement data 或直接 findings（模仿原文）。
        
- **参考范文：**  
    The meteorologists working with satellite **instruments are closing in on** the center of the typhoon, and **may** report their findings soon.

### 句子 4

**你的译文：** Many **historier** have been convinced **for** this statement though it **have existed controversy** in the better part of century

- **⚠️ 85+ 修正点：**
    
    1. **造词：** historier? 应该是 **historians**。
        
    2. **介词搭配：** convinced for 不对。be convinced **that** ... (相信某事) 或 be convinced **of** ...。
        
    3. **中式英语：** it have existed controversy 是典型的“there be”句型的误用。
        
- **参考范文：**  
    Many **historians have been convinced that** this statement is true, **for the better part of a century**, though it has remained controversial.

### 句子 5

**你的译文：** Odd though it sounds, **but** if this economic data **looks** as expected, that will be **a one more** triumph of radical theory.

- **⚠️ 85+ 修正点：**
    
    1. **逻辑连词绝症：** **虽然(Though)...但是(but)... 不能连用！** 原文 Odd though it sounds 已经是让步状语了，后面**绝对不能加 but**。这是中国学生最容易犯的语法错误。
        
    2. **主谓一致：** data 在考研学术英语中通常看作复数（单数是 datum），所以建议用 look。不过现代英语用 looks 也算对。
        
    3. **表达：** a one more 不地道 
        
        ```
        →→
        ```
        
         **yet another** (原文 yet another scientific idea)。
        
- **参考范文：**  
    **Odd though it sounds**, if these economic data **look as expected**, that will be **yet another triumph** for the radical theory.
