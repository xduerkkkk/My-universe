1. concentrating as much on technique as on outcome（既关注技巧，也关注结果）

**as much on ... as on ...** = 在……上和在……上同样多

这是 as much ... as ... 结构，表示"同等程度"。这里意思是：在技巧上投入的专注力和在结果上投入的一样多。
**值得记的句式：**

> Whatever inborn differences two people may exhibit in their abilities to memorize, those differences are **swamped by** how well each person "encodes" the information.

让步状语从句 + 主句。意思是：无论两人在记忆天赋上表现出多大差异，这些差异都会被"编码信息的能力"所压倒/淹没。swamped by = 被……淹没/远远超过。

**句 1：**

> So it is a bit confusing when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe as, "What's the difference between love and fondness?"

结构：It is confusing when… 形式主语句。field 是动词"回应"，such…as… 是"诸如…之类的"。译：所以，当 vos Savant 回应普通人诸如"爱和喜欢有什么区别"之类的问题时，就让人有点困惑了。

**句 2：**

> It's not obvious how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions that have eluded some of the best poets and philosophers.

结构：It's not obvious + how 引导的主语从句。suit sb to do = 使某人胜任。that have eluded… 是定语从句修饰 questions。译：人们看不出来，视觉化物体和推导数字规律的能力，怎么就能使一个人胜任回答那些连最优秀的诗人和哲学家都解答不了的问题。

**句 3（你标注的定位句）：**

> Scoring is now based on a statistical population distribution among age peers, rather than simply dividing the mental age by the chronological age and multiplying by 100.

译：现在的评分是基于同龄人群中的统计分布（常模），而不再是简单地用心理年龄除以实际年龄再乘以 100。核心：计分方法（computational procedure）变了。旧方法（23÷10×100=228）理论上无上限；新方法基于正态分布，最高就在几个标准差内，不可能出现 228。

**. "Today's families have budgeted to the limits of their new two-paycheck status."**

> 如今的家庭已经把预算花到了双薪收入的极限。→ 意思是两份工资全部花光，没有余量。

**2. "they have lost the parachute they once had in times of financial setback — a back-up earner (usually Mom) who could go into the workforce if the primary earner got laid off or fell sick."**

> parachute = 降落伞，比喻"安全缓冲"。破折号后是解释：过去妈妈待在家里，万一爸爸被裁员/生病，妈妈可以出去工作顶上。现在妈妈已经在工作了，这个备用方案就没了。

**3. "President Bush campaigned to move Social Security to a savings-account model, with retirees trading much or all of their guaranteed payments for payments depending on investment returns."**

拆解：

- Bush 推动把社保改成"储蓄账户"模式
- with retirees trading A for B = 退休者用 A 换 B
- A = guaranteed payments（有保障的固定支付）
- B = payments depending on investment returns（取决于投资回报的支付）
- **核心意思：把稳定的退休金变成了看股市脸色的钱，风险转嫁给个人。**

**4. "Both the absolute cost of healthcare and the share of it borne by families have risen"**

> 医疗的绝对成本上升了，家庭承担的比例也上升了（双重打击）。

**5. 最后一段长句：**

> "looks far less like an opportunity to exercise more financial responsibility, and a good deal more like a frightening acceleration of the wholesale shift of financial risk onto their already overburdened shoulders"

> 从中产家庭角度看，这一切远不像是"锻炼财务责任感的机会"，而更像是把金融风险大规模转嫁到他们已经不堪重负的肩上的一次可怕加速。
