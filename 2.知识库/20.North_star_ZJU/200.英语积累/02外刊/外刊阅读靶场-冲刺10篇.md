## 打开就能做（每篇 15—20 分钟）

1. **通读留痕（6—8 分钟）**：不查词。遇到多方博弈，在纸边标 `A / B -> 动词/立场`；遇到动词前副词（`consciously` 等）、让步词（`Certainly / True / While`）、程度与时态词（`seems / has been / more / not yet`）顺手圈出。
2. **作答三件事（6—8 分钟）**：
   - **审讯题**：逼自己分清“谁跟谁一伙”“这句话到底算不算作者观点”“抽象词具体指前文哪几个英文词（写等号）”。
   - **定位句改写打假（判断 T / F）**：**只准用指定定位句（及紧邻指代句）裁决**，严禁用通篇情绪兜底。判 **F** 时必须指出死在哪个词（程度推满 / 动作对象换了 / 范围变了 / 自己多补一环逻辑）；判 **T** 时确认它是合理的同义上位替换或正话反说。
3. **核对与标记（3 分钟）**：展开“核对”。判断错了或靠蒙撞对的小题，在篇目小标题末尾加一个 `↻`；12月14—16日只回看带 `↻` 的题，不另建统计表。
4. **灵活替换**：哪天你想读手头纸质《26外刊》，直接读纸质书并把最绕的 2 段拍进窗口让我现场考你，当天对应的这篇就顺延或留作机动。

## P01｜市政区划与住房法案

【1】State legislatures from Oregon to Massachusetts have begun overriding local zoning codes that restrict residential lots to detached single-family houses. Suburban mayors fiercely resist these mandates, warning that allowing duplexes and four-unit apartments by right will overwhelm aging sewer lines and neighborhood schools. **Certainly**, urban economists concede, upgrading municipal water pipes to serve denser blocks requires upfront capital that cash-strapped towns struggle to raise on their own. Yet decades of fiscal data indicate that sprawling single-family subdivisions actually cost towns far more per household in road and pipe maintenance than compact apartments ever do.

【2】What truly animates local opposition, housing researchers argue, is rarely infrastructure math. Public hearings on zoning reform are routinely dominated by older homeowners anxious to preserve street parking and property values, whereas renters priced out of the town altogether have no seat at the microphone. When suburban officials pledge to defend the wishes of their **constituents**, they are often responding to the loudest attendees in the room rather than the broader workforce commuting into their towns each morning. State intervention, paired with infrastructure grants, **appears** to be the most viable way to break **that political deadlock**.

1. **指称写等号**：
   - 第【2】段末句 `that political deadlock` ＝ 前文哪件事？（用原文核心英文词或准确关系写出，不写“政治僵局”四个字敷衍）

1. **定位句改写打假**（第(1)句对照【1】末句，第(2)(3)句对照【2】倒数第1、2句，判 **T / F** 并指出死因）：
   - **(1)** *Compact apartment buildings eliminate the need for towns to spend money on road and pipe maintenance.*
   - **(2)** *Suburban officials tend to prioritize the demands of vocal local homeowners over those of non-resident commuters when opposing zoning changes.*
   - **(3)** *State mandates combined with infrastructure funding have successfully resolved local resistance to denser housing.*

> [!faq]- 核对
> 1. **观点与阵营**：
>    - **作者认可这一局部事实**。句首 `Certainly` 表明作者承认城市经济学家的让步成立（升级管网确实缺钱），随后用 `Yet` 指出“低密度独栋住宅的长期维护成本其实更高”，从而驳倒郊区市长拿基础设施当挡箭牌的理由。
>    - 作者整体倾向支持 **州立法机构介入（State legislatures / State intervention）**。
> 2. **指称写等号**：
>    - `that political deadlock` ＝ 地方听证会被既有房主把持、被高房价挤出本镇的租客无话语权，导致郊区官员只顺应房主反对区划改革的困局（核心锚点：`local opposition / officials responding to the loudest attendees [older homeowners] rather than the broader workforce`）。
> 3. **改写打假**：
>    - **(1) F（程度绝对化）**：原文是独栋住宅区每户维护成本 `far more ... than compact apartments ever do`（公寓成本低得多），不是 `eliminate the need`（彻底不用花钱）。
>    - **(2) T（精准同义替换）**：`the loudest attendees in the room` 对应前句 `older homeowners`（即 `vocal local homeowners`）；`the broader workforce commuting into their towns` 对应 `non-resident commuters`；`responding to A rather than B` 对应 `prioritize A over B`。（顺带注意原文对 `constituents` 的讽刺：官员嘴上说服务“全体选民”，实际只照顾了吵得最凶的房主）。
>    - **(3) F（语气与时态推满）**：原文是 `appears to be the most viable way to break...`（似乎是最可行的破局之道，尚未全面实现），改写句坐实成了完成时 `have successfully resolved`。

## P02｜生物医学公开审稿

【1】To curb behind-the-scenes bias in academic publishing, several prominent biomedical journals now practice “transparent peer review,” publishing signed referee reports alongside accepted papers. Proponents at funding agencies argue that public accountability compels reviewers to write more constructive critiques instead of blocking rival laboratories out of spite. Early editorial audits, however, reveal an unintended behavioral shift among **junior scholars**: when asked to sign their names, postdoctoral researchers frequently decline review invitations altogether or soften methodological criticisms of manuscripts authored by **senior lab directors** who sit on future grant panels.

【2】Editors hoping to preserve frank evaluation have consequently experimented with a hybrid model—publishing the text of peer-review reports anonymously while letting referees sign only if they choose. In a two-year trial across twelve journals, **this compromise** kept refusal rates steady and exposed sloppy statistical claims to readers without forcing early-career scientists to gamble with their livelihoods.

### 先落笔再核对

1. **多角色动作图**：
   - 在第【1】段中，分别写出这三个主体的核心动作与方向：`funding agencies` → ？；`junior scholars / postdocs` → ？；`senior lab directors` → ？（谁怕得罪谁？谁掌握经费评审权？）
2. **指称写等号**：
   - 第【2】段 `this compromise` ＝ 原文哪两半具体做法？（写出英文原词动作）
3. **定位句改写打假**（只对照第【1】段最后一句 `Early editorial audits...`，判 **T / F**）：
   - **(1)** *Mandatory signature policies prompt some early-career researchers to tone down critiques of influential scientists’ papers.*
   - **(2)** *Senior lab directors frequently reject review invitations when journals require signed reports.*
   - **(3)** *Softened peer-review reports under mandatory signing have caused a surge of fraudulent medical papers in top journals.*

> [!faq]- 核对
> 1. **多角色动作图**：
>    - `funding agencies`（资助机构支持者）→ 主张公开署名能逼审稿人写建设性意见、防恶意卡竞争对手；
>    - `junior scholars / postdocs`（青年学者/博士后）→ 要求署名时，要么**拒审**（`decline review invitations`），要么**弱化对大牛手稿的方法论批评**（`soften methodological criticisms`）；
>    - `senior lab directors`（资深实验室主任）→ 是被审手稿的**作者**（`manuscripts authored by...`），同时也是未来经费评审委员会的成员（`sit on future grant panels`）。青年学者怕得罪他们。
> 2. **指称写等号**：
>    - `this compromise` ＝ `publishing the text of peer-review reports anonymously` + `letting referees sign only if they choose`（匿名公开审稿意见正文，仅自愿署名）。
> 3. **改写打假**：
>    - **(1) T（同义替换）**：`when asked to sign their names` → `Mandatory signature policies`；`postdoctoral researchers` → `early-career researchers`；`soften methodological criticisms` → `tone down critiques`；`senior lab directors who sit on future grant panels` → `influential scientists`。
>    - **(2) F（主体张冠李戴）**：共享了 `reject/decline review invitations` 和 `signed reports`，但原文拒审的主体是 `postdoctoral researchers`（青年博后），不是 `Senior lab directors`。
>    - **(3) F（自己多推一环逻辑）**：原文只说到博后会“弱化方法论批评（`soften methodological criticisms`）”，根本没往下推到“引发造假医学论文激增（`surge of fraudulent medical papers`）”。多出来的这一环是脑补。

## P03｜电子产品“维修权”之争

【1】After years of lobbying by consumer advocates, several American states have enacted “right-to-repair” statutes requiring electronics makers to sell genuine replacement batteries and screens to independent repair shops. Yet device manufacturers have blunted these laws through a software practice known as “parts pairing.” Even when an independent technician installs an authentic screen taken from an identical phone, embedded microchips trigger persistent pop-up warnings claiming the component cannot be verified, while disabling features such as automatic brightness. Consumers **alarmed by these cryptic alerts** often abandon local repair shops, even though most tell surveyors they were otherwise pleased with independent technicians’ workmanship and lower prices.

【2】Tech companies insist that parts pairing protects users from counterfeit components and safeguards biometric security. Regulators in France have tried to counter **such obstruction** by introducing a mandatory “repairability score” on product boxes, penalizing models that lock replacement parts behind proprietary software.

### 先落笔再核对

1. **心态与动作方向核查**：
   - 从第【1】段末句看，消费者放弃街边独立维修店，是因为**不满意独立维修工的手艺**吗？原文 `alarmed by these cryptic alerts` 是谁对谁施加的影响？
2. **标题二选一（只选一个最准的，并说明另一个错在哪）**：
   - **[A]** *France’s Repairability Score: A Model for Consumer Protection*
   - **[B]** *Parts Pairing: How Software Locks Undermine the Right to Repair*
3. **定位句改写打假**（对照第【1】【2】段定位句，判 **T / F**）：
   - **(1)** *Under parts pairing, even genuine components installed outside authorized networks can lose certain functions.*
   - **(2)** *Consumers turn away from independent repair shops because they doubt the technical competence of local mechanics.*
   - **(3)** *Electronics makers claim that software verification of parts helps shield users from fake components.*

> [!faq]- 核对
> 1. **心态与动作方向**：
>    - **绝不是不满意维修工手艺**（原文后半句明说 `even though most ... were otherwise pleased with independent technicians' workmanship`）。
>    - `alarmed by these cryptic alerts` 是消费者**被动受到厂商弹窗警告的惊吓**（和你在 `11年丁克篇` 记的 `be bothered ≠ not satisfied` 完全同构：被外部警告吓退 ≠ 对维修工手艺不满）。
> 2. **标题二选一**：
>    - 选 **[B]**。全文【1】【2】段主线都在讲厂商如何用 `parts pairing`（软件锁零件）架空维修权法案。
>    - **[A] 错在“把文末局部解决方案当全文主题”**（对标机场安检 `PreCheck` 篇）：法国的维修指数只是最后一句提了一嘴的应对手段，覆盖不了整篇对 `parts pairing` 机制的剖析。
> 3. **改写打假**：
>    - **(1) T**：对应【1】第3句 `Even when an independent technician installs an authentic screen ... disabling features such as automatic brightness`。
>    - **(2) F（因果与对象偷换）**：把“被系统弹窗吓到”偷换成了“怀疑本地维修工的技术能力”，与原文 `pleased with ... workmanship` 直接相反。
>    - **(3) T**：对应【2】第1句 `Tech companies insist that parts pairing protects users from counterfeit components`（`counterfeit` ＝ `fake`，`protects ... from` ＝ `shield ... from`，且保住了观点归属 `claim / insist`）。

## P04｜公共博物馆的数字版权费

【1】Nothing says “public heritage” quite like charging art historians £120 to print a thumbnail of a four-hundred-year-old oil painting whose creator died under Queen Elizabeth I. Across Britain, cash-strapped national museums routinely claim fresh copyright over high-resolution photographs of out-of-copyright masterpieces, demanding steep reproduction fees from academic presses. **Even** the Victoria and Albert Museum, an institution founded explicitly to democratize design knowledge for working artisans, long enforced intricate commercial fee schedules for archival images before partially relaxing its policy.

【2】Museum directors defend image licensing as a vital lifeline amid shrinking state subsidies. Yet financial disclosures obtained by legal scholars show that most galleries spend more on staffing licensing departments and chasing invoices than they collect from scholarly fees—making the practice a **self-defeating tollbooth** rather than a genuine revenue engine.

### 先落笔再核对

1. **无转折词句的情感与例证功能**：
   - 第【1】段第一句 `Nothing says “public heritage” quite like charging...` 字面没有转折词，作者是在夸博物馆保护遗产，还是在**反讽**？
   - 作者在第【1】段末句搬出 **`Even` the Victoria and Albert Museum**，是为了证明什么观点？（警惕：别停在例子表面讲“它旨在普及设计知识”）
2. **指称写等号**：
   - 第【2】段为什么把图片授权收费叫作 `a self-defeating tollbooth`（自讨苦吃/得不偿失的收费站）？对应哪句财务事实？
3. **定位句改写打假**（判 **T / F**；特别盯紧第(2)句的形容词！）：
   - **(1)** *The Victoria and Albert Museum is cited to illustrate how deeply entrenched image-licensing fees had become across British museums.*
   - **(2)** *The Victoria and Albert Museum is mentioned to show that charging reproduction fees was a forward-looking experiment in art education.*
   - **(3)** *Image licensing generates substantial net profits that offset declining government funding for most British galleries.*

> [!faq]- 核对
> 1. **反讽与例证功能**：
>    - **强烈反讽**（对标 `12年控烟广告 Dare to be different`）：作者死前四百年的公有领域画作，学者印张缩略图还要交120英镑，哪门子的“公共遗产”！
>    - **`Even` 信号（完全对标 `Even Mark Twain` 拍照笑题）**：连当年专门为了“向工匠普及设计知识”而建立的 V&A 博物馆都曾长期执行繁琐的收费表，说明**给公有领域藏品照片收版权费的做法在英国博物馆界有多么普遍、根深蒂固**！
> 2. **指称写等号**：
>    - `a self-defeating tollbooth` ＝ `spend more on staffing licensing departments and chasing invoices than they collect from scholarly fees`（养收费部门和催账花的钱，比向学者收上来的钱还多）。
> 3. **改写打假**：
>    - **(1) T**：精准概括例证功能（`deeply entrenched` 对应 `Even ... long enforced`）。
>    - **(2) F（过滤修饰词陷阱 + 停在例子背景细节）**：`experiment / practice` 这类名词看着中性，但前面的形容词 `forward-looking`（前瞻性的）与作者批判态度完全相反，且 `art education` 只是修饰 V&A 初衷的定语，不是举例要证明的论点。
>    - **(3) F（把辩护词当事实 + 与原文反义）**：“弥补政府拨款下降”只是馆长们的辩解（`Museum directors defend...`），【2】末句财务披露直接打脸说它入不敷出，根本没有 `substantial net profits`。

## P05｜远程办公监控软件

【1】Anxious that remote staff might be folding laundry on company time, corporate managers have flocked to tracking software that logs keystrokes, snaps webcam photos, and scores “active” screen minutes. Behavioral economists studying **these digital surveillance tools** find that they rarely boost analytical output. Instead, employees respond to constant monitoring by **consciously** staging visible busyness—nudging mouse cursors, splitting simple thoughts into a dozen Slack messages, and scheduling emails for late evening—at the expense of uninterrupted deep work.

【2】Because complex problem-solving often looks identical to staring blankly at a wall, metrics tied to keyboard activity reward performative motion while penalizing reflection. Companies that equate rapid clicking with diligence end up measuring **the theater of labor** rather than its substance.

### 先落笔再核对

1. **副词答案眼与因果链**：
   - 第【1】段末句的副词 **`consciously`** 决定了员工“拆分消息、定时发深夜邮件”是什么性质的行为？（是软件故障逼的、无意识习惯，还是员工主动刻意演出来的？）
   - 画出第【1】段末句的简短逻辑链：`持续监控` → `① ________` → `② ________`。
2. **指称写等号**：
   - 第【2】段 `the theater of labor` ＝ 第【1】段哪三个词的同义替换？
3. **定位句改写打假**（判 **T / F**，重点查谁自己多加了一环因果）：
   - **(1)** *Monitored remote workers deliberately manufacture signs of online activity, which crowds out sustained deep thinking.*
   - **(2)** *Keystroke-tracking software drives talented remote employees to quit for competitors offering flexible schedules.*
   - **(3)** *Tracking active screen minutes fails to distinguish between genuine contemplation and idle distraction.*

> [!faq]- 核对
> 1. **副词与因果链**：
>    - `consciously`（有意识地、刻意地）锁定这是**员工主动演出来的应对动作**（对标 `09年习惯篇 consciously developing new habits`）。
>    - 逻辑链：`constant monitoring` → `① consciously staging visible busyness`（刻意表演表面忙碌） → `② at the expense of uninterrupted deep work`（挤占了不受打扰的深度工作）。**只有这两环有原文支撑！**
> 2. **指称写等号**：
>    - `the theater of labor`（劳动的表演/作秀） ＝ `staging visible busyness`（或 `performative motion`）。
> 3. **改写打假**：
>    - **(1) T**：`consciously staging visible busyness` ＝ `deliberately manufacture signs of online activity`；`at the expense of uninterrupted deep work` ＝ `crowds out sustained deep thinking`。
>    - **(2) F（自己多添一环逻辑）**：常识里“监控太烦 → 优秀员工跳槽去弹性公司”特别合理，但**原文根本没写跳槽（quit）和竞争对手**！只写到损害深度思考与实质产出。
>    - **(3) T（正话反说）**：原文【2】首句说“复杂思考看起来和对着墙发呆一模一样（`looks identical to staring blankly at a wall`），键盘指标奖励表面动作而惩罚沉思”，即屏幕活跃度指标分不清“真正沉思”与“发呆摸鱼”。

## P06｜环境法规的司法审查

3. **定位句改写打假**（判 **T / F**）：

   - **(2)** *Coal-fired power companies expanded rapidly during the lawsuit by investing in affordable mercury scrubbers.*

## P07｜青少年屏幕时间研究之争

【2】In truth, both camps rely heavily on flawed self-reported screen logs: teenagers notoriously misjudge how many hours they spend scrolling, while standardized mood surveys lump fleeting boredom together with clinical depression.    - 第【1】段末句的 `inconclusive` 是什么意思？能不能等同于“证明刷手机完全无害”？
3. **定位句改写打假**（判 **T / F**）：
   - **(1)** *Longitudinal studies have proved that smartphone use has no negative impact on teenagers’ mental health.*
   - **(2)** *Current debates over teen screen time are hampered by unreliable self-reported data and imprecise mood measurements.*
   - **(3)** *Opening platform telemetry to outside scientists has resolved the disagreement between psychologists and sociologists.*

> [!faq]- 核对

## P08｜地方报纸萎缩与慈善新闻室

2. **标题范围核查（二选一）**：
   - **[A]** *The Decline of Print Newspapers in the Digital Era*
   - **[B]** *Why Charity-Funded Newsrooms Cannot Yet Replace Local Beat Reporting*
3. **定位句改写打假**（判 **T / F**）：
   - **(1)** *County newspapers have completely disappeared since classified advertising shifted online.*
   - **(2)** *Foundation-backed news outlets tend to favor sensational investigations over mundane coverage of local government meetings.*
   - **(3)** *Philanthropic donors deliberately discourage journalists from exposing statehouse corruption.*

> [!faq]- 核对

>    - **持续萎缩中**（对标 `10年报纸艺评篇 has been an inexorable decline`：现在完成时表从过去持续到现在的收缩过程，绝不能读成 `disappeared`！）。
