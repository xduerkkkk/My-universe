# 近义词·精准辨析版

> [!important] 判断标准
> 近义词不是背多组中文翻译，而是回答：**它们沿着哪一条语义轴分开？** 重点轴包括证据力度、因果方向、范围大小、程度强弱、褒贬态度、语域和搭配。

## 每组四步口答

1. 先说共同上位义。
2. 再说唯一的区分轴。
3. 每个词给一个典型搭配。
4. 指出最可能出现的命题偷换。

## 第1周｜观点、论证与因果

**日期：2026-07-27—08-02**

### 闭卷清单

1. claim / argue / contend / maintain
2. imply / infer
3. indicate / suggest / demonstrate / prove
4. cause / lead to / result in / contribute to
5. attribute / owe / account for
6. reason / cause / factor
7. opinion / view / perspective / stance
8. issue / problem / question / matter

> [!faq]- 核对
>
> | 词组 | 核心区分轴 | 精准差别 | 高频搭配 | 命题陷阱 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **claim / argue / contend / maintain** | 主张的证据强度与持续性 | claim=提出可能有争议的断言；argue=给理由论证；contend=正式而有力地主张；maintain=持续坚持某观点 | claim that；argue for/against；contend that；maintain that | 看到“作者认为”不能四词无差别替换 |
> | **imply / infer** | 信息方向 | imply=说话者/文本暗示；infer=读者依据证据推断 | the evidence implies；we infer from | 主语互换会造成推理方向错误 |
> | **indicate / suggest / demonstrate / prove** | 证据力度 | indicate=指向；suggest=较弱、暂定；demonstrate=以证据清楚显示；prove=形成决定性证明 | results indicate/suggest；data demonstrate；prove conclusively | 阅读选项常把 suggest 偷换成 prove |
> | **cause / lead to / result in / contribute to** | 因果直接度 | cause=直接造成；lead to/result in=导致结果；contribute to=只是部分促成 | cause damage；lead to/result in decline；contribute to growth | contribute to 不等于唯一原因 |
> | **attribute / owe / account for** | 归因方向 | attribute A to B=把结果A归因于原因B；owe A to B=A得益/归因于B；account for A=解释A或构成A | attribute success to effort；owe success to luck；account for the gap | 优先画出“因 → 果”再翻译 |
> | **reason / cause / factor** | 解释与成因层级 | reason=理由/解释；cause=产生结果的原因；factor=众多影响因素之一 | reason for；cause of；a contributing factor | factor 往往不能被升级为决定性 cause |
> | **opinion / view / perspective / stance** | 观点的抽象层级 | opinion=具体看法；view=较一般观点；perspective=观察角度；stance=公开立场 | in my opinion；hold the view；from a perspective；take a stance | perspective 是“从哪里看”，stance 是“站哪边” |
> | **issue / problem / question / matter** | 是否存在障碍或争议 | issue=议题/争议点；problem=需解决的困难；question=待回答的问题；matter=中性事项 | policy issue；solve a problem；raise a question；a legal matter | issue 不必然是负面 problem |

## 第2周｜研究证据、认识与变化

**日期：2026-08-03—08-09**

### 闭卷清单

1. evidence / proof / testimony / indication
2. find / discover / detect / identify
3. recognize / realize / appreciate / acknowledge
4. assess / evaluate / estimate / measure
5. precision / accuracy / validity / reliability
6. vague / ambiguous / obscure / implicit
7. change / alter / modify / transform
8. adapt / adopt / adjust / accommodate

> [!faq]- 核对
>
> | 词组 | 核心区分轴 | 精准差别 | 高频搭配 | 命题陷阱 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **evidence / proof / testimony / indication** | 证据来源与结论力度 | evidence=支持材料总称；proof=决定性证明；testimony=证人陈述；indication=迹象 | evidence for；proof of；give testimony；an indication of | evidence 通常不可数；迹象不等于证明 |
> | **find / discover / detect / identify** | 发现对象与过程 | find=一般发现；discover=首次发现未知事实；detect=察觉难以观察之物；identify=确定身份/原因 | find evidence；discover a mechanism；detect a signal；identify a risk | identify 强调“确定是什么” |
> | **recognize / realize / appreciate / acknowledge** | 从认识到公开承认 | recognize=辨认/承认存在；realize=意识到事实；appreciate=充分理解重要性；acknowledge=公开承认 | recognize a pattern；realize that；appreciate the difficulty；acknowledge a flaw | acknowledge 不等于赞同 |
> | **assess / evaluate / estimate / measure** | 判断与量化 | assess=综合判断；evaluate=按标准评定价值/效果；estimate=粗略估算；measure=实际测量 | assess risk；evaluate performance；estimate cost；measure height | estimate 不是精确 measurement |
> | **precision / accuracy / validity / reliability** | 测量质量的四个维度 | precision=重复测量接近；accuracy=接近真实值；validity=确实测到目标概念；reliability=结果稳定一致 | high precision/accuracy；valid measure；reliable test | 技术语境不可把 precision 与 accuracy 永久当同义词 |
> | **vague / ambiguous / obscure / implicit** | 不清楚的来源 | vague=边界模糊；ambiguous=有多种解释；obscure=难懂/不知名；implicit=未明说但包含 | vague idea；ambiguous wording；obscure reference；implicit assumption | implicit 不等于含糊，而是“隐含” |
> | **change / alter / modify / transform** | 变化幅度 | change=总称；alter=改变部分性质；modify=有目的的小幅调整；transform=根本转变 | change behavior；alter a plan；modify a model；transform society | modify 不能偷换成彻底 transform |
> | **adapt / adopt / adjust / accommodate** | 应对新条件的方式 | adapt=自身适应；adopt=采纳；adjust=微调；accommodate=容纳并适配他者需求 | adapt to；adopt a policy；adjust settings；accommodate differences | 最常错是 adapt 与 adopt |

## 第3周｜程度、态度与效果

**日期：2026-08-10—08-16**

### 闭卷清单

1. effective / efficient / beneficial
2. important / significant / substantial / considerable
3. common / general / universal / prevalent
4. likely / probable / possible / potential
5. skeptical / suspicious / doubtful / cynical
6. prudent / cautious / conservative / reserved
7. concern / worry / anxiety / fear
8. surprising / striking / remarkable / dramatic

> [!faq]- 核对
>
> | 词组 | 核心区分轴 | 精准差别 | 高频搭配 | 命题陷阱 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **effective / efficient / beneficial** | 结果、成本与价值 | effective=能达到目标；efficient=投入产出比高；beneficial=带来好处 | effective method；energy-efficient；beneficial effect | 有效不必高效，有效也不必有益 |
> | **important / significant / substantial / considerable** | 重要性与数量程度 | important=重要；significant=重要或统计显著；substantial=实质且量大；considerable=相当大的 | important issue；significant difference；substantial evidence；considerable cost | significant 不自动等于“巨大” |
> | **common / general / universal / prevalent** | 覆盖范围与流行度 | common=常见；general=总体/一般；universal=适用于所有；prevalent=某时地广泛流行 | common error；general rule；universal principle；prevalent belief | common/prevalent 不能升级为 universal |
> | **likely / probable / possible / potential** | 可能性与是否已显现 | likely=较可能；probable=证据上很可能；possible=存在可能；potential=尚未实现的潜在 | be likely to；probable cause；possible outcome；potential risk | possible 不能偷换成 likely |
> | **skeptical / suspicious / doubtful / cynical** | 怀疑对象与态度 | skeptical=对证据不信服；suspicious=怀疑有问题/恶意；doubtful=不确定；cynical=认为人多出于自利 | skeptical of claims；suspicious of motives；doubtful whether；cynical about politics | cynical 比 skeptical 贬义更强 |
> | **prudent / cautious / conservative / reserved** | 谨慎体现在哪 | prudent=审慎明智；cautious=避免风险；conservative=估计/行动偏低偏稳；reserved=态度不完全表态 | prudent decision；cautious approach；conservative estimate；reserved approval | 四词都不是简单“保守” |
> | **concern / worry / anxiety / fear** | 情绪强度与正式度 | concern=正式的担忧/关注；worry=日常忧虑；anxiety=持续焦虑；fear=对威胁的害怕 | public concern；worry about；anxiety over；fear of | concern 还可表示“涉及/关乎” |
> | **surprising / striking / remarkable / dramatic** | 引人注意的方式 | surprising=出乎意料；striking=视觉/认知上醒目；remarkable=非同寻常、值得注意；dramatic=变化剧烈或戏剧化 | surprising result；striking contrast；remarkable progress；dramatic rise | dramatic 常暗含幅度大，不能弱化成 merely notable |

## 第4周｜制度、责任、工作与金钱

**日期：2026-08-17—08-23**

### 闭卷清单

1. law / regulation / rule / policy
2. compliance / conformity / obedience
3. responsibility / liability / accountability / obligation
4. guilty / liable / responsible
5. job / work / career / profession / occupation
6. wage / salary / pay / income / earnings
7. fee / fare / rate / charge / toll
8. compensation / reward / return / benefit

> [!faq]- 核对
>
> | 词组 | 核心区分轴 | 精准差别 | 高频搭配 | 命题陷阱 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **law / regulation / rule / policy** | 规范来源与强制力 | law=立法机关制定的法律；regulation=主管机构的具体规章；rule=一般规则；policy=行动原则/方针 | break the law；safety regulations；follow rules；public policy | policy 不必具有 law 的法律强制力 |
> | **compliance / conformity / obedience** | 遵从什么 | compliance=按要求/法规执行；conformity=与群体规范一致；obedience=服从权威命令 | regulatory compliance；social conformity；obedience to authority | 社会从众不等于法律合规 |
> | **responsibility / liability / accountability / obligation** | 责任类型 | responsibility=一般职责；liability=法律责任/债务；accountability=需解释并承担后果；obligation=必须履行的义务 | take responsibility；legal liability；public accountability；moral obligation | liable 常涉及法律或风险，不等于负责 |
> | **guilty / liable / responsible** | 法律结论与因果责任 | guilty=被认定犯罪；liable=法律上须承担责任/赔偿；responsible=对结果负有一般责任 | found guilty；liable for damages；responsible for failure | responsible 不自动等于 guilty |
> | **job / work / career / profession / occupation** | 工作单位与长期路径 | job=具体职位；work=不可数的工作活动；career=长期职业路径；profession=需专门资格的职业；occupation=正式统计用职业 | get a job；at work；career development；medical profession；state occupation | work 通常不可数，career 不是一份 job |
> | **wage / salary / pay / income / earnings** | 劳动报酬范围 | wage=按时/件计酬；salary=固定月年薪；pay=报酬总称；income=所有收入；earnings=劳动/企业所得 | hourly wage；annual salary；equal pay；household income；company earnings | income 范围大于工资 |
> | **fee / fare / rate / charge / toll** | 收费对象 | fee=专业服务/入场费；fare=交通票价；rate=单位费率；charge=收费总称；toll=道路桥梁费或代价 | tuition fee；bus fare；interest rate；service charge；road toll | rate 是计价标准，不必是实际总额 |
> | **compensation / reward / return / benefit** | 所得的理由 | compensation=对劳动/损失的补偿；reward=对表现的奖赏；return=投资回报；benefit=获得的好处/福利 | compensation for loss；reward effort；return on investment；employee benefits | 补偿、奖赏和收益的因果理由不同 |

## 第5周｜数据、数量、趋势与关系

**日期：2026-08-24—08-30**

### 闭卷清单

1. number / amount / quantity / figure
2. rate / ratio / proportion / percentage
3. rise / raise / arise / increase
4. decline / decrease / reduce / diminish
5. tendency / trend / inclination / disposition
6. relation / relationship / correlation / connection
7. consist of / comprise / constitute / make up
8. replace / substitute / displace / supplant

> [!faq]- 核对
>
> | 词组 | 核心区分轴 | 精准差别 | 高频搭配 | 命题陷阱 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **number / amount / quantity / figure** | 可数性与数据表达 | number=可数数量；amount=不可数总量；quantity=正式“数量”，可接两类；figure=具体统计数字 | a number of cases；amount of time；large quantities；sales figures | 不要写 amount of people |
> | **rate / ratio / proportion / percentage** | 比值表达 | rate=单位时间/单位量的发生率；ratio=A:B；proportion=部分占整体；percentage=以100为基数的比例 | growth rate；sex ratio；a high proportion of；20 percent | 所有 percentage 都是 proportion，但表达形式不同 |
> | **rise / raise / arise / increase** | 词性与宾语 | rise=不及物上升；raise=及物提高/筹集；arise=问题出现；increase=可及物也可不及物 | prices rise；raise prices/funds；problems arise；increase output | rise/raise 是完形与写作高危搭配 |
> | **decline / decrease / reduce / diminish** | 下降方式 | decline=趋势性下降或拒绝；decrease=中性增减；reduce=主动使减少；diminish=程度/重要性减弱 | decline in sales；decrease by；reduce costs；diminish influence | reduce 必须有施事或被动结构 |
> | **tendency / trend / inclination / disposition** | 倾向来自哪里 | tendency=行为/现象倾向；trend=随时间的总体走向；inclination=个人偏好；disposition=较稳定性情 | tendency to；upward trend；inclination to；a calm disposition | trend 强调时间序列，inclination 强调个人意愿 |
> | **relation / relationship / correlation / connection** | 联系的性质 | relation=抽象关系；relationship=持续互动关系；correlation=统计共变；connection=一般连接 | in relation to；working relationship；strong correlation；causal connection | correlation 不能升级为 causation |
> | **consist of / comprise / constitute / make up** | 整体与部分方向 | 整体 consists of 部分；整体 comprises 部分；部分 constitute 整体；部分 make up 整体 | The team consists of/comprises ten members；Ten members constitute/make up the team | comprise 通常不用 of；先画整体和部分 |
> | **replace / substitute / displace / supplant** | 替代方向与力度 | replace A with B=用B换A；substitute B for A=用B代A；displace=把原有者挤走；supplant=逐渐取代其地位 | replace coal with gas；substitute gas for coal；automation displaces workers；supplant a theory | replace 与 substitute 的介词方向相反 |

## 第6周｜综合高频干扰项

**日期：2026-08-31—09-06**

### 闭卷清单

1. preserve / conserve / reserve / maintain
2. limit / restrict / constrain / curb / check
3. include / contain / involve / cover
4. achieve / attain / acquire / obtain
5. require / demand / call for / necessitate
6. affect / effect / influence / impact
7. alone / lonely / solitary / isolated
8. economic / economical / financial / fiscal

> [!faq]- 核对
>
> | 词组 | 核心区分轴 | 精准差别 | 高频搭配 | 命题陷阱 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | **preserve / conserve / reserve / maintain** | 保留什么状态 | preserve=保护原貌/免遭损坏；conserve=节约资源；reserve=留待以后；maintain=维持现状/运转 | preserve heritage；conserve energy；reserve seats；maintain stability | 四词都可译“保存”，对象决定用词 |
> | **limit / restrict / constrain / curb / check** | 限制来源与力度 | limit=设上限；restrict=规则限制范围；constrain=条件束缚；curb=主动遏制；check=阻止增长/发展 | limit access；restrict freedom；budget constraints；curb inflation；check power | constraint 常是结构性条件，不是主动禁令 |
> | **include / contain / involve / cover** | 整体包含关系 | include=列入部分成员；contain=内部装有；involve=必然牵涉；cover=范围涵盖 | include examples；contain water；involve risk；cover three topics | include 的列举不必穷尽，involve 强调不可避免 |
> | **achieve / attain / acquire / obtain** | 得到的对象 | achieve=完成目标；attain=达到水平/状态；acquire=逐渐获得技能/资产；obtain=取得具体物或信息 | achieve success；attain maturity；acquire skills；obtain permission | success 不能说 obtain success |
> | **require / demand / call for / necessitate** | 必要性的来源 | require=客观需要；demand=强烈要求；call for=情势要求；necessitate=逻辑上使某事成为必要 | require attention；demand action；call for reform；necessitate change | demand 含力度，necessitate 强调因果必然 |
> | **affect / effect / influence / impact** | 作用方式与词性 | affect=动词，产生影响；effect=名词结果，或动词“实现”；influence=渐进影响；impact=较强冲击/影响 | affect health；have an effect；effect change；influence behavior；economic impact | effect change 是“促成改变”，不是 affect 的拼写错误 |
> | **alone / lonely / solitary / isolated** | 客观独处与主观感受 | alone=客观单独；lonely=主观孤独；solitary=单独存在/独居；isolated=与系统或群体隔绝 | live alone；feel lonely；solitary figure；isolated community | alone 不必 lonely，isolated 强调断联 |
> | **economic / economical / financial / fiscal** | 经济语域 | economic=经济体系的；economical=节省成本的；financial=钱、融资、企业财务；fiscal=政府税收与预算 | economic growth；economical car；financial market；fiscal policy | economic policy 与 fiscal policy 范围不同 |

## 选项排错标签

| 标签 | 判断问题 | 典型例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 范围升级 | 原文只是部分/可能，选项是否改成全部/必然？ | contribute to → cause；possible → certain |
| 程度升级 | 原文是迹象，选项是否改成证明？ | suggest → prove |
| 因果倒置 | 因和果、归因方向是否颠倒？ | attribute A to B |
| 语域偷换 | 同一中文词是否属于不同制度/学术语域？ | policy → law；rate → percentage |
| 褒贬改变 | 审慎怀疑是否被升级为愤世嫉俗？ | skeptical → cynical |
| 搭配不成立 | 词义近但语法方向是否错误？ | replace A with B / substitute B for A |
